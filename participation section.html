PARTICIPATIONS

Hardware Fellowship for Locus 2025

"Completed an eight-day Hardware Fellowship for LOCUS 2025, organized by the Robotics Club at IOE Pulchowk Campus. Explored topics like basic electronics, Arduino microcontrollers, sensors, motors, and PCB designing. The fellowship culminated in building an Autonomous Wall-Avoiding Bot, integrating sensors, motors, and Arduino coding. This experience significantly expanded my knowledge in electronics, coding, and practical applications."

First HACKATHON

"Participated in IS HACKATHON 2024, a 36-hour innovation marathon organized by the Information System Club at KUSOM. Our team, SAAAS, worked on the theme of unemployment. The event, held from June 14-16, 2024, at KUSOM, Balkumari, was an incredible experience. We learned how to solve societal problems with unique solutions, saw outstanding presentations, and had excellent team coordination. It was a phenomenal opportunity to grow our network and build a project together."

One-day Python Workshop

"Participated in a one-day Python workshop organized by the Nepal Open Source Klub (NOSK) in sponsorship with Programiz. Learned the basics of Python, which fostered my interest in diving deeper into the language."

Three day Linux workshop
